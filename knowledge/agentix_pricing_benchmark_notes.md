# Agentix Labs AI — Pricing Benchmark Notes

**Date:** 2026-09-21  
**Purpose:** Owner/internal pricing research only. Do not vectorize this file into the customer-facing RAG knowledge base.

## Important

Agentix Labs AI does not currently have a verified public fixed-price list on its website. The price ranges in the canonical RAG knowledge base are recommended sales bands derived from current 2026 market benchmarks plus Agentix Labs AI's positioning as a Pakistan-based custom AI engineering company.

They are not claims about historical client charges.

## Pakistan-market signals reviewed

- A Pakistan AI-agent provider publicly lists a simple single-channel AI agent around PKR 50,000 and an omnichannel package around PKR 150,000, with enterprise work custom quoted.
- A Pakistan automation provider lists starting prices around PKR 60,000 for LLM integration, PKR 75,000 for an AI support bot, PKR 85,000 for a WhatsApp sales agent, and PKR 95,000 for a RAG knowledge base.
- Another Pakistan AI-services provider lists process automation from about PKR 80,000, AI-powered systems from about PKR 200,000, larger AI transformation work from about PKR 500,000, and monthly support from about PKR 50,000.
- A Pakistan chatbot pricing guide places many custom chatbot builds roughly in the PKR 50,000–200,000 range, with voice usage incurring separate recurring API cost.
- Pakistan custom software benchmarks vary much more widely and increase strongly with modules, integrations, platforms, and engineering seniority.

## International-market signals reviewed

- 2026 voice-agent pricing guides commonly place focused proofs of concept around USD 8,000–25,000 and established-agency production voice systems around USD 35,000–150,000+, plus usage costs.
- 2026 RAG pricing guides range from several thousand dollars for smaller custom builds to tens of thousands or more for integrated production systems.
- 2026 custom AI application guides commonly place prototypes in the tens of thousands of dollars and production products significantly higher.
- 2026 computer-vision pricing guides commonly place focused MVPs around USD 15,000–30,000 and advanced production systems substantially higher.
- Pakistan/offshore engineering is commonly priced below US/UK agency rates, so Agentix does not need to copy Western-enterprise pricing to remain commercially credible.

## Pricing strategy used for the RAG knowledge base

The recommended Agentix bands intentionally:
- sit above commodity freelancer/no-code pricing;
- remain below mature Western enterprise-consultancy rates;
- preserve room for discovery and scoping;
- separate development fees from recurring API/telephony/cloud usage;
- price advanced voice, multi-agent, full-stack AI, and computer-vision work higher than simple workflow automation;
- avoid pretending that one fixed package can represent every custom AI system.

## Recommended policy

Use ranges for qualification, not as automatic quotes.

For serious prospects:
1. understand the workflow;
2. identify integrations and data;
3. estimate usage and risk;
4. decide whether a pilot or production build is appropriate;
5. issue a scoped proposal.

Do not promise discounts, fixed timelines, or included third-party usage unless explicitly approved.
